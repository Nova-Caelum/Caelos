Nova Caelum — Reference

Surface Treatments

Canonical reference for the three NC surface patterns. Every visual layer that goes into production must derive from one of these three treatments.

Pattern 01

Gridline Background

28 px crosshatch via CSS linear-gradient. Color: Deep Slate Violet (#3D426C) at 12 % opacity on Deep Midnight Indigo. Grid reads at arm's length; disappears behind body text. Floating text is acceptable only for hero/sparse copy — dense text must use a card (see Pattern 02).

Floating text on grid — acceptable for sparse or hero copy

page bg shown at normal scale

Pattern 02

Card Over Grid

Opaque raised surface (#1a1828) that fully occludes the grid. Use for any block with dense or important text. Pairing: grid bg behind, card above — text never competes with grid lines. This is not a glass surface; opacity is intentional.

Clarity of purpose shapes every engagement

We work with founders and operators who are building something worth building — and who need a thinking partner, not just a vendor. Our practice is small by design: fewer clients, deeper work, longer commitments.

Pattern 03

Liquid Glass

Two canonical variants. Both require -webkit-backdrop-filter (Safari). For callouts and overlays only — not for dense body text over grid. The blur effect reads best when colorful or textured content sits behind the surface.

Variant A — Dark

Navigation & overlays

rgba(29,19,41,0.80) + blur(24px). Source: Header.tsx sticky nav. Deep Midnight Indigo at 80 % occludence — strong anchoring, still lets depth show through.

Variant B — Light-on-dark

Content panels & callouts

rgba(245,235,221,0.06) + blur(16px). Source: ChatbotBar.tsx chat panel. Dawn Cream at 6 % — a whisper of warmth on the dark surface; very low opacity by design.
